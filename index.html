Questions people ask first

When do I get my cash back?

Cash back is paid as a statement credit, so it lowers what you owe directly. The $100 welcome bonus is added once you reach $1,000 in purchases within your first 90 days.

Is there a limit on the 2%?

The 2% rate applies to the first $15,000 of grocery, gas and bill purchases each year, which is $300 back. After that those purchases earn 1%, like everything else.

How do I avoid paying interest?

Pay your full statement balance by the due date. You get at least 21 days after each statement, the minimum Canadian law requires. Cash advances start charging 22.99% right away.

How does the rate drop work?

Make 12 monthly payments on time in a row and your purchase rate drops from 20% to 19%. Payment history is about 35% of your credit score, so the same habit raises your score too.

What credit score do I need?

We look for 650 or higher. On TransUnion's scale that is a good score; Equifax starts its good range at 660.

Will checking my eligibility hurt my score?

No. The check on this page only compares your answers with our requirements. A full application includes a credit check.

What happens if my card is lost or stolen?

Lock it in the Northlight app straight away. Visa Zero Liability means you are not responsible for purchases you did not make, and new purchases have 90 days of protection against theft or damage.

Can my credit limit go up?

Yes. After six on time payments we review your limit. Keeping your balance under 30% of your limit also helps your credit score.
